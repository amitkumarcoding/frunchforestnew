The Nutrient Almanac

A short field guide to what's actually inside every handful — the vitamins, minerals and healthy fats that almonds, cashews, walnuts, raisins and fox nuts quietly carry to your plate.

Specimen Index

Six nutrients worth knowing

NO. 01PROTEIN

Protein

The building block your body uses to repair muscle and stay full for longer.

AlmondsCashews
Read the field note

A small handful of almonds or cashews carries a meaningful dose of plant protein alongside fibre, which is why they curb hunger far better than a snack of refined carbs. Pair them with a fruit or yoghurt for a more complete amino acid profile.

NO. 02OMEGA-3 FATS

Healthy Fats

Not all fat is equal — these are the kind that support heart and brain health.

Walnuts
Read the field note

Walnuts are one of the few plant sources with a genuinely useful amount of alpha-linolenic acid, a plant-based omega-3. Regularly including a few walnuts in the diet is commonly associated with better cholesterol balance and steadier energy through the day.

NO. 03DIETARY FIBRE

Fibre

Keeps digestion moving and helps even out blood sugar swings after meals.

RaisinsAlmonds
Read the field note

Because raisins concentrate the fibre of fresh grapes into a smaller bite, a small portion goes a long way toward daily fibre needs. Combined with almond skins, which carry their own fibre and antioxidants, this pairing is a simple way to support gut health.

NO. 04IRON

Iron

Essential for carrying oxygen through the blood and staving off fatigue.

RaisinsCashews
Read the field note

Raisins and cashews both carry non-heme iron, the plant-based form the body absorbs best when paired with vitamin C. A squeeze of lemon over a trail mix, or eating them alongside citrus, helps the body take up more of it.

NO. 05MAGNESIUM

Magnesium

A quiet mineral behind muscle recovery, sleep quality and steady mood.

CashewsFox Nuts
Read the field note

Cashews are one of the more magnesium-dense nuts, and roasted fox nuts (makhana) add their own light, low-fat contribution. Together they make an easy evening snack that doesn't sit heavy before bed.

NO. 06ANTIOXIDANTS

Antioxidants

Plant compounds that help the body manage everyday cellular wear and tear.

WalnutsRaisins
Read the field note

The thin brown skin on a walnut and the dark skin of a raisin both concentrate polyphenols — so resist the urge to peel them. Left whole, both snacks do double duty as flavour and quiet cellular support.
